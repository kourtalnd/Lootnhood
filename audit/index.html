      What it checks

      Dev track record

      Every prior launch from the deployer wallet and how many graduated. A wallet on its ninth launch with zero graduations is a different bet than a proven dev.

      The dev's own trades

      Whether the dev bought or sold on their own bonding curve before graduation, and how much they still hold after it.

      Holder concentration

      Top holder and top-10 share with the curve itself excluded — who can dump on you, and how much they hold.

      Bundle & snipe patterns

      Wallets that bought in the launch block, and whether one buyer took an outsized share of the curve.

      Creator tax

      The per-trade fee the dev skims, read from the curve's own trade events — including when it's set high enough to be the business model.

      Copycat tickers

      Whether the symbol copies a token that already graduated — a classic impersonation setup.
